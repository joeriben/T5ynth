fm3 — Parameterkombination

three-operator phase modulation: TWO modulators driving one carrier's phase in linear combination, so the spectrum carries two sideband families and the drive travels back and forth BETWEEN them. That travel is what two operators cannot do -- with one modulator a control can only make its single family stronger or weaker, never trade it for a different one. Method: Chowning, JAES 21(7), 1973; the topology and the word-to-parameter directions from Hayes & Saitis 2020, thirty sound designers on exactly this layout. Csound 6.18 has no generic N-operator opcode, so the two modulators are summed into a `phasor` and read through `tablei`. BJ's reading of the setting this was found at, 2026-07-30: bassoon-like, with a kind of pulse-width modulation -- the `fm` entry beside it reads Rhodes-like.
